## Idea 1: Quiet Luxury Minimalism (Linear/Stripe inspired)

**Design Movement:** Quiet Luxury Minimalism — inspired by Linear.app, Stripe, Vercel

**Core Principles:**
1. Radical restraint — every element earns its place
2. Typography as the primary visual element
3. Asymmetric tension through deliberate imbalance
4. Negative space as a premium signal

**Color Philosophy:** Pure #FAFAFA background with #0A0A0A text. No color accents — the absence of color IS the luxury statement. Borders use subtle #E8E8E8. The only "color" is the contrast between black type and white space.

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric sections — alternating left-heavy and right-heavy content blocks. Headlines break the grid intentionally. Large section numbers (01, 02...) as structural anchors. Content never centered unless it's a CTA.

**Signature Elements:**
- Oversized section numbers in light gray as background texture
- Thin horizontal rules (1px) as section dividers
- Monogram logo: geometric W from two overlapping V shapes

**Interaction Philosophy:** Interactions are whisper-quiet. Hover states are barely perceptible — a 1px border shift, a slight opacity change. Nothing screams. The site communicates confidence through stillness.

**Animation:** Framer Motion viewport-triggered fade-in with slight upward translate (y: 20 → 0). Stagger children by 80ms. Duration 0.6s ease-out. No bouncy springs — only smooth, deliberate motion.

**Typography System:**
- Headlines: Bricolage Grotesque (variable weight 400–800) — editorial, slightly quirky
- Body: DM Sans (400, 500) — clean, readable, modern
- Hierarchy: 72px hero → 48px section → 32px subsection → 16px body
